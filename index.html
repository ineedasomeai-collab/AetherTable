🔒 Per-Element Lock & Multi-Layered FX

When stacking multiple effects together (e.g. fire over firewood, smoke over fire, or swimming fish inside a pond):

  • Press L or check "🔒 Lock Position & Size" in the Attributes popup to anchor any effect.
  • Click-Through Protection: Once locked, pointer clicks pass straight through the effect so you can freely select, place, and drag other effects above or below it without disturbing your placement!
  • Accident-Proof: Locked effects cannot be accidentally dragged, rotated, resized, or deleted with the Delete key.
  • Selecting Locked Elements: Use the Active Map Element Switcher (◀ Prev / Next ▶ / Dropdown) in the Attributes popup to re-select any locked element and modify attributes or unlock it.
  • Bulk Controls: Use 🔒 Lock All and 🔓 Unlock All in the Realistic FX panel to lock the entire table when players place physical miniatures.

🗺️ Scale & Move with Map (Map Anchoring)

Effects can live in Map Space (pinned to the battle map) or Screen Space (stationary relative to the display screen):

  • Scale & Move with Map (Default: ON): Anchors the effect to the exact spot on the battle map. When you pan across a dungeon or zoom into a room, the campfire, river, or fallen tree pans and zooms in lockstep with the artwork!
  • Screen Space (OFF): The effect stays stationary on screen (perfect for fullscreen ambient rain, blizzard flurries, or tactical HUD reticles).
  • 1:1 Cursor Tracking: Dragging or resizing an effect while the map is zoomed in (e.g. 2.0x) or out (0.5x) automatically scales mouse movement so handles follow your cursor with 1:1 precision.
  • Bulk Pinning: Click 📌 Pin All to Map in the Realistic FX panel to anchor all placed overlays at once.

✋ Map Panning & Navigation (No Center-Lock)

  • ✋ Pan Mode (P key): Click the Pan Mode button in the bottom HUD or in the FX panel. Left-dragging anywhere on the battle map moves the map freely across your screen.
  • Right-Click Drag: Right-click and drag anywhere on the canvas to pan smoothly at any time in any mode.
  • Arrow Keys: Use keyboard arrows (or Shift+Arrow for fast speed) to nudge the map across large 4K battlemaps.
  • Zoom Controls: Use + and - buttons or mouse wheel to inspect details, and ⌖ Reset View (R) to recenter.
  • Smart Drop Positioning: Dropping any of the 375 Realistic FX automatically places it right at the center of your current panned view.
